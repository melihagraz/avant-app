# Privacy Policy — Avant Dating App

Last updated: March 23, 2026

## Data We Collect
- Email address (for authentication)
- Profile information (name, age, city, preferences)
- Profile photos
- Messages between matched users

## How We Use Your Data
- To create and manage your account
- To match you with compatible users via AI agents
- To send notifications about new matches

## Data Storage
Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers located in the United States.

## Data Deletion
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen.
